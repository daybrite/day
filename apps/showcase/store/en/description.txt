Day Showcase is the demonstration app for Day, a framework for building apps in Rust that draw themselves with each platform's own native widgets.

Everything you see here is real UIKit on iPhone, real Material Components on Android, real AppKit on a Mac — driven by a single Rust codebase, with no web view and no custom rendering.

WHAT IS INSIDE

• Controls — buttons, toggles, sliders, pickers and text fields, each bound two ways to a signal
• Text and text areas — styling, selection, and editing
• Canvas & shapes — paths, gradients, transforms and gestures
• Grid, List, Tabs and Stack — the native layout and navigation containers
• Menus & dialogs, Toolbars, Preferences — the platform's own chrome
• Date & time, Localization, Resources — formatting and assets that follow the system
• Device & sensors — battery, connectivity and live accelerometer, gyroscope and magnetometer traces
• Platform services, Focus, Animation, Web View, Map, Refresh and Crash reporting

The whole app is translated into English, French, Arabic and Simplified Chinese, and switching language at runtime relays every screen — including the right-to-left layout Arabic requires.

WHO IT IS FOR

Developers evaluating Day, and anyone curious what a Rust app looks like when it uses the real thing instead of imitating it. It is a catalog, not a product: there is nothing to sign in to and nothing to buy.

Day is open source. The code for every screen in this app is at github.com/daybrite/day.
